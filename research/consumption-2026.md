# Consumption and other taxes in Poland in 2026

*State of the law as of **27.11.2026**. Research date: 27.11.2026. Machine-readable version: `consumption-2026.json`.*

**How to read the source tags**
- **[P]** means the value was checked by reading the primary text: a Dziennik Ustaw or Monitor Polski PDF, a URE/KRRiT notice, or an official municipal page.
- **[S]** means the value comes from a secondary source (news site, portal or price-comparison site). Use it only as a cross-check or a locator.
- **[E]** means our own estimate or assumption, used only in worked examples.

## 0. The most important 2026 facts (read first)

1. **Fuel taxes were cut temporarily in 2026, and the cut is in force on 27.11.2026.** The government's "Ceny Paliw Niżej" package came in response to the Middle East conflict. It has three parts:
   - **VAT on petrol and diesel is 8% instead of 23%.** Periods: **31.03–30.06.2026**, **17.08–31.08.2026** and **3.10–31.12.2026**. Between those periods the rate was 23% (1.07–16.08 and 1.09–2.10). Legal basis: rozporządzenie MF z 9.12.2023 r. w sprawie obniżonych stawek VAT (Dz.U. 2023 poz. 2670), § 11a, 11b and 11c, added by Dz.U. 2026 poz. 417, 573, 642, 699, 771, 1095 and 1288. [P]
   - **Excise on petrol is 1 239 zł/1000 l instead of 1 529, and on diesel 880 zł/1000 l instead of 1 160.** Periods: **30.03–15.06.2026** and **3.10–31.12.2026**. Legal basis: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 416, 506, 572, 641, 698, and for the current period **Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1289**. [P]
   - **Maximum pump price for Pb95, Pb98 and diesel**, announced almost daily in Monitor Polski under art. 34a ustawy o zapasach ropy naftowej (added by Dz.U. 2026 poz. 415). It applies only while the reduced VAT applies. [P]
   - **LPG is not covered.** LPG keeps 23% VAT and the full 659 zł/1000 kg excise.
2. **The 2026 sugar fee has not changed:** 0,50 zł + 0,05 zł/g + 0,10 zł, capped at 1,20 zł/l.
   - An increase was vetoed in December 2025. [S]
   - A second bill (0,70 / 0,10 / 1,00, cap 1,80 zł/l, from 1.01.2027) passed the Sejm on 5.11.2026 and **is awaiting the President's decision**. [S]
3. **Excise increases under the excise "map" came into force on 1.01.2026.**
   - Alcohol: +5%.
   - Cigarettes: +20% on the per-1000-sticks part.
   - Fine-cut tobacco: +30%.
   - E-liquid: 0,96 → 1,44 zł/ml.
   - Nicotine pouches: 150 → 200 zł/kg.
4. **Electricity bills:**
   - The household price freeze has ended. The average URE tariff price for 2026 is 495,16 zł/MWh net.
   - Opłata OZE rose from 3,50 to 7,30 zł/MWh.
   - The capacity fee (opłata mocowa) rose.
   - Opłata przejściowa is reported to have ended in 2026. [S]
5. **Already enacted for 2027, so a "2027" rate file is needed:**
   - Alcohol excise of about +20%. [P]
   - Cigarettes 476,10 zł/1000 sticks. [P]
   - Opłata paliwowa 2027. [P]
   - Abonament RTV 2027. [P]
   - E-cigarette excise changes. [P]

## 1. VAT (ustawa o VAT, tekst jedn. Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1263) [P]

**Rates.** The basic rate is **23%** and the reduced rate is **8%**. Under art. 146ef these apply for as long as defence spending exceeds 3% of GDP, so they are in force in 2026 (the statutory text of art. 41 still says 22% and 7%). The second reduced rate is **5%** (art. 41 ust. 2a, załącznik nr 10). Exports and intra-EU supplies are **0%**. The main exemptions are in art. 43 ust. 1.

**2026 changes:**
- The VAT rate on fuels changed temporarily (see section 0).
- The subjective exemption limit rose from 200 000 to **240 000 zł** from 1.01.2026 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 896). This matters for small service providers, who can sell without VAT.
- KSeF (e-invoicing) became mandatory in 2026. It has no effect on rates.

- For 2025 it was **1 037,72 zł/1000 sticks**, which is 20,75 zł per pack of 20 (Obwieszczenie MFiG, M.P. 2026 poz. 205).

## 4. Sugar fee and the "małpki" fee

**Opłata cukrowa 2026** (ustawa o zdrowiu publicznym art. 12a–12f, tekst jedn. Dz.U. 2026 poz. 149) [P]. The fee has been unchanged since 2021.
- **0,50 zł/l** for sugars ≤5 g/100 ml, or for any sweetener.
- **+0,05 zł** for each gram of sugar above 5 g/100 ml, rounded up to a whole gram.
- **+0,10 zł/l** for caffeine or taurine.
- **Cap: 1,20 zł/l.**
- Drinks with ≥20% juice, and isotonic drinks, pay only the 0,05 zł/g part.
- Pending: a bill with 0,70 / 0,10 per g / 1,00 for caffeine, cap 1,80 zł/l and concentrates at 3 zł/l, from 1.01.2027. It awaits the President's decision. [S]

**Opłata za małpki** (art. 9² ust. 11 ustawy o wychowaniu w trzeźwości, Dz.U. 2023 poz. 2151) [P]:
- **25 zł per full litre of 100% alcohol** in packages of **≤300 ml**.
- Unchanged in 2026. podatki.gov.pl page dated 15.12.2025.
- A proposal for 100 zł/l from 2027 exists but is not enacted. [S]

### Opłata śmieciowa (waste fee; ustawa o utrzymaniu czystości, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 733, art. 6k ust. 2a) [P]

| Method | Cap | Cap from 31.03.2026 |
|---|---|---|

| Per m² of flat | 0,08% | 2,80 zł/m² |

### PCC (tax on civil-law transactions; ustawa o PCC, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 191) [P]

- **2%** on the sale of real estate or movables, incl. **used cars** and secondary-market flats (art. 7 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. a).
- **Exempt: first home.** A natural person buying a flat or house who has never owned one (art. 9 pkt 17) pays no PCC.
- **Exempt:** movables with a taxable value of ≤1000 zł (art. 9 pkt 6).
- Primary-market flats bought from a developer carry 8% VAT instead of PCC.

### Capital and gambling

- **Podatek Belki (tax on interest, dividends and capital gains): 19%** (art. 30a ust. 1 PIT, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 592). [P]
- **Gambling tax** (ustawa o grach hazardowych art. 74, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 595) [P]. The operator pays it on stakes:

  | Game | Rate |
  |---|---|
  | Lotteries (gry liczbowe, e.g. Lotto) | **20% of stakes** |
  | Cash lotteries | 15% |
  | Raffle lotteries | 10% |
  | Sports betting | **12% of stakes** |
  | Slot machines and casino games | 50% of GGR |
  | Bingo, telebingo, poker tournaments | 25% |

  On top of that, a **dopłata** is charged to players in state-monopoly games: **25% of the stake** in lotteries and 10% in cash lotteries and telebingo (art. 80). Example: on each 1 zł of Lotto stake, the player pays 1,25 zł, and 0,45 zł of it (≈36%) goes to the state as tax plus dopłata.
- **Tax on winnings: 10%** (art. 30 ust. 1 pkt 2 PIT). **Exempt** if a single win in lotteries, betting, promotional or raffle lotteries is **≤2 280 zł** (art. 21 ust. 1 pkt 6a lit. a). [P]

## 8. Worked examples (formulas)

Notation:
- P = gross retail price.
- VAT share of gross = P × r / (1 + r).
- Excise and fees are per unit.
- Excise and fees sit inside the VAT base, so VAT is also charged on them.

### 8.1 One litre of Pb95, 27.11.2026 (reduced regime)

Inputs:
- P = 5,81 zł.
- VAT = 5,81 × 8/108 = **0,430**.

## 9. Unverified or ambiguous items

1. **Opłata przejściowa = 0 in 2026.** Source is the Ministry of Energy communication via media. The legal basis was not read.
2. **Sugar fee 2027 bill.** It awaits the President's decision (Sejm vote 5.11.2026; the 21-day deadline is about late November). The status may change any day.
3. **Minimum excise on cigarettes.** The weighted average price for Jan–Oct 2025 (art. 99 ust. 5d) was not found. We used the full-year 2025 value, 1 037,72 zł. The effective minimum per pack (15,68 zł) is approximate.
4. **Heated tobacco ad valorem base.** Which weighted average price of fine-cut tobacco applies in 2026 (the 2024 or the 2025 value, 838,51 zł/kg) was not confirmed.
5. **Retail prices** for cigarettes, vodka, beer and cola are **estimate ranges**. No primary 2026 source was found. The GUS average-price database (dbw.stat.gov.pl) should be queried directly. The bread price (5,57 zł) is GUS data quoted by a secondary source.
6. **Electricity all-in price per kWh** varies by network operator (0,97–1,32 zł/kWh in various portals). The 200 zł bill example assumes 175 kWh per month.
7. **Kraków waste fee of 35 zł.** Taken from media reports from 2025. The 2026 resolution was not read.
8. **LPG density** of 0,54 kg/l is an assumption (it ranges from about 0,51 to 0,56).
9. **Plastic bag fee and VAT.** MF says VAT applies on top. There is court case-law against this. Check whether the 2019 regulation has been amended.
10. **Bottled water at 23%.** Inferred from the annex (only tap or cistern water is listed at 8%). Confirm with an MF binding rate information (WIS) or a tax explanation if it will be shown to users.
11. **2026 excise changes in mid-year (fuel)** are fully verified. VAT 8% on fuel is likely contrary to the EU VAT Directive (Commission statement, [S]) but is legally in force in Poland.
12. **The car excise taxable value** and **small-brewery beer rates** were not modelled.
13. **Abonament RTV exemptions** (75+, disabilities) were not re-verified for 2026.
